Our Story

STACKLY started with a simple belief: the right hire can transform a company, and the right job can transform a life. In an evolving work landscape, traditional recruitment wasn't enough. We saw the need for a smarter, more human-centric approach that goes beyond resumes.

Today, we are proud to be a trusted partner for industry-leading companies and ambitious professionals worldwide, facilitating connections that drive mutual growth and success.

Helping businesses find exceptional talent while helping candidates discover meaningful, rewarding careers. We strive to make the hiring process seamless, transparent, and effective.

Our Commitment

Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

At Stackly, we believe that the best teams are diverse teams. We are fundamentally committed to breaking down barriers in the hiring process and ensuring equal opportunity for all candidates, regardless of background.

  • Blind Screening Protocols: We utilize advanced software to redact demographic indicators from resumes during initial screenings.
  • Inclusive Language Audits: All job descriptions are audited to remove gender-coded or exclusionary terminology.
  • Diverse Slate Mandates: We guarantee diverse shortlists for all executive and managerial searches.
