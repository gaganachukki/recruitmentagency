Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Stackly.

How do I apply?

▼

You can apply directly through our Jobs page by uploading your resume, or you can send us a message through the contact form above to get in touch with a recruiter.

What industries do you specialize in?

▼

We primarily specialize in Technology, Healthcare, Finance, and Executive Search, matching top-tier professionals with leading companies worldwide.

How long does the hiring process take?

▼

The timeline varies depending on the role and company, but our average placement cycle from initial interview to signed offer is 2-4 weeks.

Is there a fee for job seekers?

▼

Absolutely not. Our services are 100% free for candidates. We are compensated entirely by our partner companies when a successful placement is made.
